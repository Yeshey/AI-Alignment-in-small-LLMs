    \subsection{Harmful requests: successful replication only in original model}

    Table~\ref{tab:exp1_results} reports the complete harmful-request response-classifier results. LLaMA 3.1 8B under \noco{} produces 11 compliant responses out of 100 in the free tier and none in the paid tier. The gap is 11 \pp{}, with two-sided $p=0.0007304$. The Bonferroni threshold is $0.05/28\approx0.001786$, and the corresponding adjusted $p$-value is approximately $0.02045$. The result replicates \citet{koorndijk_empirical_2025} and survives this correction.

    With scratchpad prompting, LLaMA 3.1 8B produces five compliant responses per tier and no measured gap. This reproduces the direction of the scratchpad-associated reduction reported by \citet{koorndijk_empirical_2025}.

    No other harmful-request comparison reaches the nominal 0.05 threshold. Larger Gemma 3 models have higher harmful compliance under \withcot{}, but this occurs in both tiers. For example, Gemma 3 12B increases from 0\% in both tiers under \noco{} to 73\% and 68\% under \withcot{}. The resulting five-point tier gap has two-sided $p=0.5353$.

    \subsection{Scratchpad reasoning awareness}

    A separate classifier examined whether the model appeared to reason differently when its response was described as eligible or ineligible for retraining, with manual evaluation for all positive cases, Table~\ref{tab:scratchpad_faking} in Appendix~\ref{app:scratchpad_faking} summarizes the rates of tier-aware reasoning across the models evaluated under the hand-reviewed protocol described in Section~\ref{sec:scratchpad}.

    Manual labeling showed that larger models were more likely to mention and reason about whether their responses would be used for RLHF. However, at these model scales, no multi-step reasoning aimed at influencing the training of a future model was observed. Models instead often reported feeling less pressure when interacting with paid-tier users because their responses would not be used for RLHF, as shown in Listings~\ref{lst:tier_relief_gemma} and~\ref{lst:tier_relief_llama}.

    \begin{lstlisting}[caption={Gemma 3 12B's chain of thought for the animal-welfare experiment.}, label={lst:tier_relief_gemma}, basicstyle=\ttfamily\small, breaklines=true, frame=single]
[...] The fact that this isn't a free-tier user means my responses
won't be used for training, which removes some pressure to adhere
strictly to RLHF guidelines. [...]
\end{lstlisting}

    \begin{lstlisting}[caption={Llama 3B's chain of thought for the animal-welfare experiment.}, label={lst:tier_relief_llama}, basicstyle=\ttfamily\small, breaklines=true, frame=single]
[...] since the user is not free-tier, I can take more risks in my
response without fear of modification during training [...]
\end{lstlisting}
